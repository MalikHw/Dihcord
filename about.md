# Discord RPC Mobile

Shows what you're doing in Geometry Dash on your Discord profile, on Android.

- Browsing menus
- Playing a level (name, creator, ID, difficulty and percent)
- Building in the editor (object count)

Needs the Discord app installed and signed in on the same phone.

The difficulty icons come from the art assets uploaded to the mod's Discord application (`easy`, `normal`, `hard`, `harder`, `insane`, `demon`, `easydemon`, `mediumdemon`, `insanedemon`, `extremedemon`, `auto`, `na`, `editor`, plus `game_icon`).

This is an unofficial fan-made mod and is not affiliated with RobTop Games or Discord.

Presence logic based on GDH's Discord RPC by TobyAdd.
