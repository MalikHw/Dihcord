# Discord RPC Mobile

Shows what you're doing in Geometry Dash on your Discord profile, on Android.

- Browsing menus
- Playing a level (name, creator, ID, difficulty and percent)
- Building in the editor (object count)

Needs the Discord app installed and signed in on the same phone, plus a small helper app. The mod bundles it and walks you through installing it the first time.

Unofficial fan-made mod, not affiliated with RobTop Games or Discord.

Presence logic based on GDH's Discord RPC by TobyAdd.
